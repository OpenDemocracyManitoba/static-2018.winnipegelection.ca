Seven Oaks - 2

Voters may select up to four school trustee candidates on the election ballot.

This ward is in the far north of Winnipeg, and includes the Amber Trails, The Maples, Leila North Leila-McPhillips Triangle, Rosser-Old Kildonan, Riverbend and Rivergrove neighbourhoods. It also includes the portion of Mandalay West to the east of the rail line. Prior to the 2018 civic election, this ward was represented (in alphabetical order) by Diane Cameron, Derek Dabee, Greg McFarlane, and Maria Santos.

A ward map is available here.

As of January 1st, 2017, the Seven Oaks School Division had 7,519 early/middle-school students (Kindergarten to Grade 8) and 3,992 high school students (Grades 9 to 12).

Public schools within the Seven Oaks School Division include:
