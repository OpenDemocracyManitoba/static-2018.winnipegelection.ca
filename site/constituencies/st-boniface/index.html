St. Boniface

This ward is located in the east central and southeast parts of Winnipeg. The ward boundaries will have some changes that will take effect for the 2018 civic election. The new ward has lost seven of its neighbourhoods on the east and south sides to the Transcona and St. Vital wards.

Under its previous boundaries the ward had a population of 59,801 residents, based on 2016 census data. The new ward has a population of 47,174 residents, based on 2016 census data.

Prior to the 2018 election boundaries, all neighbourhoods within the new boundaries of this ward were represented by Councillor Matt Allard, who was elected in 2014. The Dugald, St. Boniface Industrial Park, Symington Yards, and Southland Park neighbourhoods will now be part of the Transcona ward. The Royalwood, Fraipont, and Sage Creek neighbourhoods will now be part of the St. Vital ward.

Neighbourhoods within the new ward boundaries include:

  • Archwood
  • Central St. Boniface
  • Dufresne
  • Holden
  • Island Lakes
  • Maginot
  • Mission Industrial
  • Niakwa Park
  • Niakwa Place
  • North St. Boniface
  • Norwood East
  • Norwood West
  • Southdale
  • Stock Yards
  • The Mint
  • Tissot
  • Windsor Park

New ward population and neighbourhoods retrieved from the 2017 Winnipeg Wards Boundaries Commission Final Report.
