Fort Rouge - East Fort Garry

This ward is located in south central Winnipeg, including the downtown core area. The ward boundaries have had no changes from the 2014 civic election. The ward population is 46,770 residents, based on 2016 census data. Prior to the 2018 election, this ward was represented by Councillor Jenny Gerbasi, who was elected in 1998, 2002, 2006, 2010, and in 2014.

Neighbourhoods within the new ward boundaries include:

  • Armstrong Point
  • Broadway-Assiniboine
  • Crescent Park
  • Earl Grey
  • Ebby-Wentworth
  • Legislature
  • Lord Roberts
  • McMillan
  • Point Road
  • River-Osborne
  • Riverview
  • Roslyn
  • South Portage
  • The Forks
  • West Broadway
  • Wildwood

New ward population and neighbourhoods retrieved from the 2017 Winnipeg Wards Boundaries Commission Final Report.
