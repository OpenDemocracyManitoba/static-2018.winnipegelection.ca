Daniel McIntyre

This ward is located in west central Winnipeg. The ward boundaries have had no changes from the 2014 civic election. The ward population is 46,882 residents, based on 2016 census data. Prior to the 2018 election, this ward was represented by Councillor Cindy Gilroy, who was elected in 2014.

Neighbourhoods within the new ward boundaries include:

  • Central Park
  • Colony
  • Daniel McIntyre
  • Minto
  • Portage-Ellice
  • Sargent Park
  • Spence
  • St. Matthews
  • Wolseley

New ward population and neighbourhoods retrieved from the 2017 Winnipeg Wards Boundaries Commission Final Report.
