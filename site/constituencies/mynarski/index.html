Mynarski

This ward is located in north central Winnipeg. The ward boundaries will have some changes that will take effect for the 2018 civic election. The new ward has obtained three additional neighbourhoods from the Point Douglas ward.

Under its previous boundaries the ward had a population of 42,393 residents, based on 2016 census data. The new ward has a population of 49,808 residents, based on 2016 census data.

Prior to the 2018 election boundaries, all neighbourhoods within the new boundaries of this ward east of Arlington Street were represented by Councillor Ross Eadie, who was elected in 2010 and in 2014. The Mynarski, Robertson, and the portion of Dufferin neighbourhood west of Arlington Street, were part of the Point Douglas ward, represented by Councillor Mike Pagtakhan, who was also elected in 2002, 2006, 2010, and in 2014.

Neighbourhoods within the new ward boundaries include:

  • Dufferin
  • Dufferin Industrial
  • Inkster-Faraday
  • Jefferson
  • Kildonan Park
  • Lexton
  • Lord Selkirk Park
  • Margaret Park
  • Mynarski
  • North Point Douglas
  • Robertson
  • Seven Oaks
  • St. John’s
  • William Whyte

New ward population and neighbourhoods retrieved from the 2017 Winnipeg Wards Boundaries Commission Final Report.
