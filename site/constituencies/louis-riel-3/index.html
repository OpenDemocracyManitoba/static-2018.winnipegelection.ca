Louis Riel - 3

Voters may select up to three school trustee candidates on the election ballot.

This ward is in the far south part of Winnipeg, and includes the Minnetonka, Vista, Meadowood, River Park South, Dakota Crossing, and Normand Park neighbourhoods. It also includes the portion of the St. Vital Perimeter South neighbourhood to the west of the Seine River. Prior to the 2018 civic election, this ward was represented (in alphabetical order) by Josie Landry, Sandy Nemeth, and Dave Richardson.

A ward map is available here.

As of January 1st, 2017, the Louis Riel School Division had 10,086 early/middle-school students (Kindergarten to Grade 8) and 4,934 high school students (Grades 9 to 12).
